Privacy Policy

Gridnest

com.gridnest.appEffective 13 November 2026

In short

Gridnest asks for no personal information, has no account or sign-up, and runs no analytics. Your levels, streak, Dots pictures, hints and settings are saved only on your own device. We have no server that could store anything about you.

The puzzles are made on your phone, so the game works fully offline. The only outside services are Google Play (for an optional subscription) and Google AdMob (for optional video ads and short ad breaks), both described below.

Local only — never transmitted

What is stored on your device

  • Game progress — the newest level you reached, the Daily puzzles you solved, your streak and any days you saved, Dots pictures finished and their stars, and your guesses.
  • Hints — how many free, earned and bonus hints you have today.
  • Ad timing — when the app was installed and when the last ad break played, so ads stay spaced out.
  • Subscription status — whether Gridnest Pro is active.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently. There is no backup on our side, so a reinstall starts fresh.

Two destinations, neither of them ours

What leaves your device

Google Play billing

If you buy Gridnest Pro, the whole transaction is handled by Google Play (or Apple's App Store on iPhone). The app never sees your name, email or payment details — it only learns whether a subscription is active.

Google AdMob

Used for the optional video ads and the short ad breaks described under Advertising. Pro subscribers see no ads.

Optional rewards, never required to play

Advertising

How ads appear. You can choose to watch a short video ad for an extra hint, to open a missed Daily puzzle, or to keep a streak you missed yesterday. Free players may also see a full-screen ad break between puzzles — never in the first day after installing, never more often than every few minutes, and never for Pro subscribers. There are no banner ads.

Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. To show an ad and prevent fraud, Google receives technical information such as your IP address, device type and operating system, an advertising identifier, and whether the ad was viewed. This is collected and controlled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us. We receive no personal data from Google — the app is told only whether an ad loaded and whether a reward was earned.

Ad requests ask for non-personalised ads only and are limited to a family-friendly content rating. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings under Google › Ads.

Printing and sharing pictures

A finished Dots picture can be printed or saved as a PDF. The sheet is made on your phone and handed to your device's own print service or share sheet; nothing is uploaded to us. Whatever printer or app you choose handles the file under its own policies.

Children's privacy

Gridnest is a puzzle game for a general audience. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. There are no profiles, no chat, no user-generated content and no way for one user to contact another.

Permissions the app requests

InternetLoading ads and processing subscriptions. The puzzles themselves need no connection.
VibrateHaptic feedback while drawing.

Advertising IDUsed by Google AdMob as described above.

Some additional permissions may appear in the app's technical listing because they are declared by the frameworks the app is built on rather than requested by the app itself. Gridnest does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything — uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your device settings.
  • Manage a subscription — cancel or change it at any time in the Play Store or App Store subscription settings.

Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on request.
