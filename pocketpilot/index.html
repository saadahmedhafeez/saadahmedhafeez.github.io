PocketPilot

Budget and expense tracker for Android and iPhone

Snap a receipt, say what you bought, or type it the way you'd text a friend: PocketPilot sorts every item into the right category with its price, reminds you before bills are due, and coaches you to save every month.

  • Scan shop receipts and utility bills; every line is read with its price and the bill's own date.
  • Add expenses by voice or text, in any currency.
  • Calendar, monthly summary, category limits and a money coach.
  • Bills and subscriptions with reminders; a shopping list; loans person by person.
  • Your expenses stay on your phone. Optional backup goes to your own Google Drive (the app can only see the files it creates there).
