Privacy Policy

Trivium Daily

com.triviumdaily.appEffective 1 November 2026

In short

Trivium asks for no personal information, has no account or sign-up, and runs no analytics. Everything you do in the app — your streak, level, XP, unlocked categories and settings — is saved only on your own device, and we have no server that could store anything about you.

The only ads are optional: after the free stages of a Learn section you can watch a short video ad to open one more stage. They are delivered by Google AdMob and described under Advertising below.

Local only — never transmitted

What is stored on your device

The app saves a small amount of data using your device's local storage. This never leaves your phone and is not readable by us or by anyone else:

  • Game progress — your XP, level, daily streak, unlocked tiers and best scores, stages you opened with an ad, the answers so far in a stage you left part-way, questions you got wrong (for the practice deck), accuracy per topic, solved riddles, and the scores of your recent matches against the computer opponent.
  • Settings — light or dark appearance, haptics, and whether the daily reminder is on.
  • Category preferences — which picture categories you chose to show.
  • Onboarding flag — a single value recording that you have seen the intro, so it is not shown twice.
  • Cached questions — the current day's trivia questions, so the app works offline and does not re-download them.
  • Subscription status — which tier, if any, is active.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently. There is no backup on our side and nothing to restore, so a reinstall starts fresh.

Four destinations, none of them ours

What leaves your device

The app makes network requests to exactly four places. We operate none of them, and we receive no personal data back from any of them.

Open Trivia Database (opentdb.com)

Text trivia questions and the category list are fetched from the Open Trivia Database, a free public API. These requests ask only for a number of questions and an optional category or difficulty. No information about you is included.

As with any internet request, their server necessarily sees your device's IP address and standard connection details in order to reply. That data is handled under their policies, not ours. Picture rounds, riddles and the Kids Learning Zone are bundled into the app and need no network access at all.

Wikipedia (api.wikimedia.org)

For the daily "On this day" history spark and history questions, the app asks Wikipedia's public feed for events that happened on a given calendar date, and may load the small picture Wikipedia shows for an event. The request contains only the month and day. The app identifies itself as "Trivium" and sends nothing about you. Wikimedia sees your IP address as with any web request; that is covered by the Wikimedia Foundation's privacy policy.

Google Play billing

If you buy a subscription, the entire transaction is handled by Google Play. The app never sees or handles your name, email, or payment details — it only receives back whether a subscription is currently active. Google's handling of that purchase is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob

An ad is requested only when you choose to watch one to open a stage. See Advertising below.

Third-party components

The app is built with the Expo and React Native frameworks and includes these Google components. We list them because they ship inside the app even though we do not use them to gather anything about you:

  • Google Play Billing — required to offer subscriptions. Active only when you open the subscription screen or make a purchase.
  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) — used only for the optional video ads that open extra Learn stages.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — included automatically by the notifications library the app uses. The app never registers for push notifications and has no push server, so no messages are ever sent to your device from us. This component is present but unused.

The app contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK of any kind.

Optional, and never required to play

Advertising

Ads are optional. Each Learn section has free stages. After those, a locked stage can be opened with a Pro subscription or by choosing to watch one short video ad. No ad ever plays unless you tap to watch it, and there are no banner or pop-up ads.

These ads are delivered by Google AdMob. To show an ad and to prevent fraud, Google receives technical information from your device such as your IP address, device type and operating system, an advertising identifier, and whether the ad was viewed or completed. This is collected and controlled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, not by us. We receive no personal data from Google — the app is told only that a reward was earned.

Because the app includes a Kids Learning Zone, ad requests are configured restrictively: ads are limited to the most conservative content rating, requests are flagged as child-directed and as being for users under the age of consent, and personalised (interest-based) advertising is switched off. Ads never appear anywhere inside the Kids Learning Zone.

You can reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings under Google › Ads.

Notifications

The app can show one notification a day, the Daily Spark (a fact, riddle or small puzzle), which you can switch off in Settings. It is scheduled locally by your own device and contains no data from anywhere else.

Permission is requested once. If you decline, notifications are simply skipped and every other feature continues to work normally. You can change your mind at any time in your device's app settings.

Kids Learning Zone

Children's privacy

The app includes a Kids Learning Zone with questions written for ages 4–12. This section deliberately sits outside the rest of the app's systems: no online play, no advertising, and no subscription prompts, and leaving it requires a grown-up to answer a sum or press and hold.

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either. We do not knowingly collect personal information from any user regardless of age. There is no profile, no messaging, no user-generated content, and no way for one user to contact another.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything — uninstall the app, or clear its storage from your device settings. Both wipe all local data immediately and completely.
  • Turn off notifications — decline the prompt, or revoke it later in device settings.
  • Manage a subscription — cancel or change it at any time through the Play Store's subscription settings.

Because we hold no data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export, correct, or delete on request — the controls above are complete in themselves.
