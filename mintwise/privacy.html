Mintwise Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 November 2026

Mintwise ("the app") identifies coins from photos. This policy explains what data the app uses.

Photos

When you scan a coin, the photo is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI service only to identify the coin and estimate its value. We do not use your photos for advertising, sell them, or share them with anyone else. Your scanned photos and collection are stored on your device.

Account and purchases

The app creates an anonymous ID (no name, email or login) so we can count free scans and check your subscription. Purchases are handled by the App Store or Google Play and our payment partner RevenueCat; we never see your card details.

Camera and photo library

The camera is used only to photograph coins. The photo library is opened only when you choose a picture to scan.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting data

Delete scans in the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored on your device. To delete server data linked to your anonymous ID, email us (below).
