Mintwise: Coin Identifier — delete your data

Mintwise (developer: Saad Ahmed Hafeez) has no accounts. Your scanned coins and photos are stored only on your phone.

Delete data on your phone

  1. Open Mintwise and delete scans from a coin's page, or
  2. Uninstall the app to remove all scans and photos from your phone.

Delete data on our server

Our server keeps only an anonymous app ID, how many free scans it used, and whether it has an active subscription. To delete it:

  1. Email saadi.ahmed94@gmail.com with the subject "Mintwise data deletion".
  2. We delete the data linked to your app within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Subscriptions are managed by Google Play or the App Store; cancel them in your store account settings.
